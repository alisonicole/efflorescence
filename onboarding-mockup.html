1 of 5 · Your Garden

Tend your flowers daily.

Each habit is a flower. Tap it to water it. Long-press for a rest day. Your streak grows the plant from seed to bloom.

2 of 5 · Journal Tree

Every entry is a leaf.

Your journal grows into a tree. Each entry is a leaf you can tap to re-read. The more you write, the fuller your tree becomes.

3 of 5 · Ground

When it gets too loud.

Tap GROUND any time things feel overwhelming. It walks you through a short grounding practice using your senses.

4 of 5 · Daily Check-In

Name what's real.

Each day, name where you are. Your spiral unlocks a journal prompt written exactly for that feeling. You don't have to face it alone.
