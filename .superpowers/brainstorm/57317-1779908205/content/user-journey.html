01 — The Healing Arc
Five seasons. One direction.
Healing from a significant relationship loss follows a measurable arc. The app is designed around this arc — not to rush it, but to meet you exactly where you are at each stage.
Late Autumn
Days
1–14
Shock. Acute grief. Numbness and rawness alternating.
The nervous system is in crisis mode. Dopamine withdrawal is physiologically identical to withdrawal from cocaine.
Common spirals
The Replay The But He I Don't Know
Active features
Daily check-in No-contact habits Diary The Why Ground
Seeds in the soil. Nothing visible yet. The work is happening underground.
Winter
Days
15–30
The replay begins. Comparison, longing, questioning self-worth.
Acute distress begins to modulate. Memory is being edited and reconstructed with every replay.
Common spirals
The Clock The Mirror The What If
Active features
Rewrite Room Full Picture Inspire (sci) Habit streaks
Sprouts breaking ground. Effort is still required. The pattern is forming.
Early Spring
Days
31–60
Beginning to surface. More okay days. Still fragile.
Approaching the 66-day automaticity threshold. Habits begin requiring effort to skip rather than effort to do.
Common spirals
The Mirror Actually Okay The Clock
Active features
Full Picture Blossoms Inspire (notes) Milestones
Stems rising. Buds forming. The garden is visibly growing.
Full Spring
Days
61–90
Consistency has arrived. Glimpses of joy. Identity beginning to re-emerge.
Automaticity threshold crossed. Emotional charge on memories measurably decreasing. Less emergency, more past tense.
Common spirals
Actually Okay The What If
Active features
Grove view Calendar Custom habits Inspire (milestones)
Flowers open. Petals multiplying. The garden is blooming.
Summer
Days
91+
Rediscovery. The relationship is real history, not active emergency. Identity is your own again.
New behavioral pathways are established. The loss occupies a different register — meaningful, not consuming.
Common spirals
Actually Okay All
Active features
Full garden Grow habits Journey view Day 90 milestone
Full bloom. Radiant. This was always coming.
02 — The Daily Loop
What a day in the app looks like.
The app is designed around two touchpoints per day — morning intention and evening reflection — with crisis support available any time in between.
When it happens
Morning
Check in — name the spiral, set intent
30 seconds. No journal required.
Morning
Tend the garden — water one or more habits
Each tap grows the flower. Streak accumulates.
Anytime
Journal — diary, full picture, rewrite, the why
Write when you need to. Nothing is required.
Anytime
Inspire — science card matched to your spiral
Context for what your body is doing right now.
Crisis
Ground — 4-7-8 breathing, or read The Why
Before you text him. Before the spiral takes over.
Evening
Optional second check-in

03 — Feature Discovery
When each feature becomes meaningful.
Not every feature is equally relevant on day 1. This is the natural discovery timeline as the user moves through healing stages.
Day 1
Foundation
Healing start date. Default habit seeds. The Why written. First check-in.
Days 1–7
Daily rhythm
Check-in habit forms. Flowers go from seeds to sprouts. First streaks begin.
Day 7
First blossom
First 7-day streak possible. Blossom earned. Grove begins. Inspire feed unlocks.
Days 14–21
Full Picture
Enough distance from the relationship to see it clearly. Full Picture + Rewrite Room most active.
Day 21
Milestone 1
First milestone card. Pattern exists. Neural pathway has begun forming.
Day 30
Milestone 2
Acute distress begins modulating. Journey calendar shows a month of evidence.
Day 60
Automaticity
Milestone 3. Approaching 66-day threshold. Behaviors require effort to skip.
Day 90
Radiant
Milestone 4. Garden is radiant. Emotional charge measurably decreased. Full bloom.
